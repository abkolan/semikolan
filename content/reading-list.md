No rating system and no particular order. Listing a book here is how I
force myself to finish it and stick to the reading habit.

## Reading

- **[Thinking in Systems: A Primer](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/3828902-thinking-in-systems)** - _**Donella Meadows**_. Re-reading this, asking Claude to challenge me after every chapter. Been fun so far, but making sense of the claudisms, not so much.

- [**Why Machines Learn:** The Elegant Math Behind Modern AI](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/195888801-why-machines-learn) - **_Anil Ananthaswamy_**. A phenomenal book that just explains the math behind everything around us.

- [**Inference Engineering**](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/252701214-inference-engineering) - **_Philip Kiely_**. If you look past the marketing speak for baseten.com, this is a great book to learn about inference.

- [**Notes from Underground, White Nights, The Dream of a Ridiculous Man, and Selections from The House of the Dead**](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/17876.Notes_from_Underground_White_Nights_The_Dream_of_a_Ridiculous_Man_and_Selections_from_The_House_of_the_Dead) - **_Fyodor Dostoevsky_**. I'm on my "re-read all your favourite classics" arc. This time reading the Signet Classics edition, with the fantastic translation by Andrew MacAndrew.

## Slow burns

- [**A Philosophy of Software Design**](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/39996759-a-philosophy-of-software-design) - **_John Ousterhout_**. The clearest argument I've read that complexity is the enemy and deep modules are the cure. Short, opinionated, correct.

- [**Scaling People:** Tactics for Management and Company Building](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/79332047-scaling-people) - **_Claire Hughes Johnson_**. Lessons from a person I admire, at companies I admire. Love the cover too!
